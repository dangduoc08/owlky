Golden flower bed April 14, 2025

A sunny bed of golden flowers lights up the garden from spring to autumn. Plant it once and it comes back every year with a little care.

Method:

Pick a spot with at least six hours of sun. Loosen the soil to a spade's depth and mix in a layer of compost.

Set the plants a hand's width apart, firm the soil around each one and water them well.

Water once a week while they settle in, then only in dry spells.

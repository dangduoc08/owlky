Chart: stock charts (ADR-0047). `indicators` computed in core (SMA, EMA, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, Donchian and Keltner channels over the price; volume, RSI, MACD, stochastic, ATR, CCI and PVT in panes of their own; HTML `data-ok-indicators`), panes on one time axis (`paneHeight`), a `navigator` that moves and resizes the zoom like a brush, a volume per point (`v`, a Volume column), indicator columns in the data table and one tooltip readout of every pane. The indicator math is exported (`smaValues`, `rsiValues`, `macdValues`…).

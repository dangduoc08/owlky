Add Avatar and AvatarGroup, Badge (counts, dots and presence statuses), Chip (filled, outlined and soft; clickable and deletable) and Label. In HTML, broken avatar images fall back to a letter, `data-ok-size` sizes avatars, and a chip's delete button fires a cancelable `ok:chip:delete`.

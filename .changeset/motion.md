Add Motion, MotionGroup, MotionText, CountUp and MotionBorder. Motion plays entrance and exit animations (slide, fade, zoom, bounce, flip, scale and rotate) when content scrolls into view, on mount or when you show it, plus looping backgrounds (Ken Burns, pan and color change), SVG stroke drawing and a hover and press effect. Content stays visible without JavaScript, and reduced motion shows it at once. Dialog gets a `transition` option that plays a Motion entrance on its paper. New tokens: `--ok-duration-enter`, `--ok-duration-exit` and `--ok-easing-expressive`.

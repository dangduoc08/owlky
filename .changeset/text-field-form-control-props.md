Text field: the FormControl props `hiddenLabel` (`data-ok-hidden-label`: the label is visually hidden but still names the input, and the field is laid out as if it had none, so a filled field gets less padding above the value), `focused` (`data-ok-focused`: the focus colors without focus) and `component` (the root element). InputAdornment takes `disablePointerEvents` (`data-ok-disable-pointer-events`: presses go through to the input) and `component`.

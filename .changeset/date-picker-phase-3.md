Date pickers phase 3 (ADR-0028): range pickers. `DateRangeCalendar` (one to three months, hover preview, drag editing of the ends with a mouse or a finger), the `DateRangePicker` family (desktop, mobile, static), `TimeRangePicker` and `DateTimeRangePicker` (desktop, mobile), single and multi input range fields for dates, times and date times, range shortcuts for dashboard filters and `invalidRange` validation, in React and HTML. HTML values are ISO 8601 intervals (`2025-05-20/2025-05-25`); new runtimes `date-range-calendar`, `multi-input-range-field` and `date-range-picker`, and `data-ok-range` single input fields on the `date-field` runtime.

Data grid: select row ranges as in MUI X. With checkbox selection, Shift + click on a row or its checkbox selects every row from the last toggled one, Shift + Space toggles the focused row, and Shift + Up / Down extends the selection (or shrinks it over selected rows). Core adds `gridRowRangeSelection`, `gridRowArrowSelection` and the navigation's `onRowRange`.

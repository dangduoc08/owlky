Select: `startAdornment` and `endAdornment` (MUI InputBase). An `InputAdornment` sits inside the field box: the start one before the value (it keeps the InputLabel above the field and opens the outline's notch), the end one before the arrow. Native selects take them too. In HTML, put `[data-ok-text-field-adornment]` before the trigger or after the arrow icon.

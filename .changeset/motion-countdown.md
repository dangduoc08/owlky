Motion: Countdown. React `Countdown` shows the days, hours, minutes and seconds left until `date` (a `Date`, an ISO string or ms since the epoch), or of a number of `seconds`, with `largestUnit` (`days` · `hours` · `minutes` · `seconds`, larger units fold into it), `paused` (a pause control for WCAG 2.2.2: seconds keep their time left, a date shows the real time left when it resumes), `now` (the time of the first render, so server and client markup agree; without it a date countdown renders `--` until it mounts), `labels`, `completeLabel` ("Time's up"), `onTick(remaining)` and `onComplete()`; change the `key` to start over. The root has the `timer` role (not live) and a visually hidden polite status region that announces the time left at each whole hour, each whole minute, at 30 and 10 seconds, and at the end, instead of every second. HTML: `span[data-ok-countdown]` with `data-ok-date`, `data-ok-seconds`, `data-ok-now`, `data-ok-largest-unit`, `data-ok-paused`, `data-ok-complete-label`, `[data-ok-countdown-unit]` › `[data-ok-countdown-value]` + `[data-ok-countdown-label]`, `[data-ok-countdown-status]`, a `countdown` runtime with `ok:countdown:tick` (`{ remaining }`) and `ok:countdown:complete` and `{ remaining, paused, pause(), resume(), restart() }`, and the `data-ok-countdown-toggle` (mirrors `aria-pressed`) and `data-ok-countdown-restart` triggers. Core: `countdownOptions` / `countdownAttributes`, `createCountdown`, `countdownParts`, `countdownValueText`, `countdownText`, `countdownStep`, `countdownAnnouncement`, `countdownInitial`, `parseCountdownTime`, `parseCountdownSeconds`. Styles: `countdown.css`. Motion gets a "Countdown" story (Date with a pause toggle, Seconds with a Start button) and docs section.

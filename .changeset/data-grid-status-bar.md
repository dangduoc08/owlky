Data grid: a status bar for cell selection. `statusBar` (HTML `data-ok-status-bar` and a `div[data-ok-data-grid-status-bar][role=status]` in the markup) shows the count of the selected cells and the sum, average, min and max of their numbers, through the aggregation functions of those names. New locale keys `statusBarCount`, `statusBarSum`, `statusBarAvg`, `statusBarMin`, `statusBarMax` (every locale pack). Core: `gridSelectionStats`, `gridStatusItems`.

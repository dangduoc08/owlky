Design styles: the Bauhaus and Brutalism styles as opt-in sheets (`@owlky/styles/styles/bauhaus.css` and `brutalism.css`, CDN `owlky-style-bauhaus.min.css` and `owlky-style-brutalism.min.css`, about 2.6 kB gzip each). Bauhaus is flat with 2px ink edges, square shapes and pill chips, Montserrat with capital headings and labels, and a blue and yellow palette on warm greys. Brutalism is flat with 1px ink lines and square shapes, IBM Plex Mono for every family, and a blue and orange palette on warm greys. Tokens: `bauhaus` and `brutalism` are exported, and `styleNames` lists them.

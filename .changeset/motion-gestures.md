Motion gestures (roadmap 5.3, ADR-0062), with Framer Motion's names. Drag: `drag` (`true`, `"x"`, `"y"`), `dragConstraints` (a box in px, an element ref or an element `id`), `dragElastic` (default 0.5), `dragMomentum` (an inertia glide from the release velocity, springing back from constraints), `dragSnapToOrigin`, `dragDirectionLock`, `dragTransition` (`power`, `timeConstant`, `bounceStiffness`, `bounceDamping`, `restDelta`, `restSpeed`, React `modifyTarget`), and `onDragStart` / `onDrag` / `onDragEnd` (`point`, `delta`, `offset`, `velocity`), `onDirectionLock`, `onDragTransitionEnd`. Arrow keys move a focused draggable Motion (10 px, Shift 50 px) and report the same events (WCAG 2.5.7). Spring states `whileHover` (mouse and pen), `whileTap` (also Enter and Space on the Motion or a button inside it), `whileFocus` (`:focus-visible`) and `whileDrag`, layered focus < hover < tap < drag, with `onHoverStart` / `onHoverEnd` / `onTapStart` / `onTap` / `onTapCancel`; a state's own `transition` key, else the Motion's `transition`, else springs for moves and scales. Reduced motion: the content still follows the pointer, releases settle at once and states jump. HTML: `data-ok-drag`, `data-ok-drag-constraints`, `-elastic`, `-momentum="false"`, `-snap-to-origin`, `-direction-lock`, `-transition`, `data-ok-while-hover|tap|focus|drag` (JSON) and the `ok:motion:dragstart`, `drag`, `dragend`, `directionlock`, `dragtransitionend`, `hoverstart`, `hoverend`, `tapstart`, `tap`, `tapcancel` events; `data-ok-dragging` while dragged. Core: `createMotionGestures`, `createDrag`, `trackPan`, `inertiaPath`, `panVelocity`, `dragRanges`, `motionGestureAttributes`, `readMotionGestures` and a shared swipe helper `createSwipe`. Styles: draggable Motions get `cursor: grab` and the `touch-action` of their axis. Motion gets a "Gestures" story and docs sections.

Drawer: swipeable drawers (MUI `SwipeableDrawer`, roadmap 4.18.46). React `DrawerContent swipeable` with `swipeAreaWidth` (20), `hysteresis` (0.52), `minFlingVelocity` (450), `disableSwipeToOpen`, `disableDiscovery` and `disableBackdropTransition`; a touch or pen swipe towards the edge closes the drawer and one from a strip along the edge of the screen opens it, reported as `onOpenChange(open, "swipe")`. HTML: `data-ok-swipeable` and the same options as attributes on a temporary dialog, with `<div data-ok-drawer-swipe-area aria-hidden="true">` right after it; a swipe to open fires `ok:drawer:beforeopen` (cancelable, `trigger: null`) and `ok:drawer:open`, a swipe to close `ok:drawer:beforeclose` with the reason `swipe`. Core: `createDrawerSwipe`; `DrawerCloseReason` gains `"swipe"`. Drawer gets a "Swipeable" story and docs section.

Design styles: the Editorial and Minimal Mono styles as opt-in sheets. Load `@owlky/styles/styles/editorial.css` (CDN `owlky-style-editorial.min.css`) or `minimal-mono.css` after `owlky.css` and set `data-ok-style` on any element. Editorial is flat and square: Noto Serif Display 900 headings, Source Serif 4 text, Archivo labels in capitals, 1px ink edges on surfaces, a rose accent on warm greys (about 2.7 kB gzip). Minimal Mono uses greys for the primary and secondary colors, radius 8 / 14px, soft grey shadows and Inter (about 2.1 kB gzip). Tokens export `editorial` and `minimalMono`.

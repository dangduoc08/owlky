Chart crossfilter between the charts of a group (ApexCharts Premium Linked Views / Highlight Filter, ADR-0054). Set `crossfilter` (`data-ok-crossfilter`) to `"filter"` or `"highlight"` on charts that share a `group`: a selection made with a click, Enter or Space (or a brush chart's brush) filters the group by its categories, matched by position. `filter` charts draw only the filtered categories' values (circular charts hide the other slices; the value axis fits what is left), `highlight` charts tint them behind the series (`g[data-ok-chart-filter-layer] › [data-ok-chart-filter-band]`); legends, data tables and categories keep the whole data. Every participant renders `div[data-ok-chart-filter]` after the plot with a **Clear filter** button (`[data-ok-chart-filter-clear]`, hidden while the group has no filter) and a visually hidden polite status (`[data-ok-chart-filter-status]`) where the chart you act on announces "Filter: North, West" or "Filter cleared" (`localeText` `clearFilter`, `filter`, `filterCleared`). Clearing clears the group's filter and the selection that set it. React: `onFilterChange(categories, own)`. HTML: the `ok:chart:filter` event (detail `{ categories, own }`) and the instance's `filter` and `clearFilter()`. The link registry (`linkChart`) keeps one filter per group (members' `filter` / `cleared`, links' `filtered` / `clearFilter`); core exports `chartFilterCategories`, `chartBrushCategories`, `chartFilter`, `chartFilterText`, `chartFilterLabels`, `chartFilterNodes`, `filterChartData`, `chartFilterHidden`, `chartCrossfilters` and the `ChartCrossfilter` / `ChartGroupFilter` types; `renderChart` takes `crossfilter` and `filter`. Charts without `crossfilter` render as before. New story: Crossfilter.

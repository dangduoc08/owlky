Add Checkbox, Switch and FormControlLabel: native checkboxes with every palette color and `default`, `medium` / `small` sizes, indeterminate and custom icons (Checkbox), `edge` (Switch) and four label placements. In HTML, a checkbox with `aria-controls` checks or clears the listed checkboxes and shows a mixed state. Table selection columns use small checkboxes, and a switch with `data-ok-table-dense="<table id>"` turns dense rows on and off.

DataGrid phase 6: row grouping (column menu, nested groups, also through a data source), aggregation (`sum`, `avg`, `min`, `max`, `size`, with a footer row), pivoting (a panel for rows, columns and values), Excel (.xlsx) export, clipboard copy and paste, cell range selection, header filters and a chart that follows the rows (Chart component). Tree data now generates rows for parents that have no row of their own, and date filters use the DatePicker. Column menus offer Group by and Aggregation items, and the Export menu offers Download as Excel.

MotionBorder (beta): a palette or `inherit` color now draws a soft light that runs along the border over a faint track. The new `secondaryColor` option (`data-ok-secondary-color`) adds a second light that starts on the opposite side. Per-side border widths and per-corner radii follow your CSS. Additive: no existing option, attribute or default changed.

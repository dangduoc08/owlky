Add Link, built on Typography: theme colors (or the parent's color), the type scale, and an underline that is always shown, shown on hover or hidden. A link without a destination renders a button, `component` renders a router link, and links that open a new tab always get `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Breadcrumbs examples now compose Link.

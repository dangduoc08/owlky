Organization chart: `direction` (`data-ok-direction`) grows the chart from the `top` (default), `bottom`, `left` or `right`, in CSS only and with the same markup. `left` and `right` are logical, so right-to-left pages mirror them; collapse toggles sit on the edge that faces the children and their arrow points at them. Reading and focus order stay the tree order.

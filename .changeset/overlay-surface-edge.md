Popovers, menus, Select and Autocomplete lists, picker popovers and Walktour steps keep the style's surface edge: in the flat styles (Swiss, Bauhaus, Brutalism, Editorial) they no longer melt into the page without a shadow or an outline, and Editorial outlines them whole, like dialogs. The popover arrow sits outside the edge. Soft and the other styles without a surface edge look the same. Tooltips keep their filled look without an edge.

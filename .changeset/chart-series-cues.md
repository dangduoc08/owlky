Chart non-color series cues (WCAG 1.4.1; ApexCharts `markers.shape`, `markers.discrete`, `fill.type: "pattern"` / `fill.pattern.style`, `stroke.width` / `stroke.dashArray` per series, `theme.accessibility.colorBlindMode`): `markerShape` (`circle`, `square`, `triangle`, `diamond`, `cross`, `plus`, `star`) shapes the markers of every series, hover markers too; `fill="pattern"` fills bars, areas and pie, donut and polar area slices with a different pattern per series or slice, and `fillPattern` (`slantedLines`, `squares`, `circles`, `horizontalLines`, `verticalLines`) picks one; `strokeWidth` sets the line width; `colorBlindMode` gives each series its own shape (lines get markers), pattern and dash. Series take `markerShape`, `fillPattern`, `strokeWidth` and `dashArray` of their own (HTML: `data-ok-marker-shape`, `data-ok-fill-pattern`, `data-ok-stroke-width`, `data-ok-dash-array` on the column header). `discreteMarkers` (`{ seriesIndex, dataPointIndex, color, size, shape }`, HTML `data-ok-discrete-markers="0 4 error star 8"`) marks single points. Legend and tooltip markers repeat the shape or pattern (`data-ok-shape`, `data-ok-pattern`). Patterns are SVG `<pattern>` definitions with ids unique per chart, colored by the palette tokens. HTML attributes: `data-ok-marker-shape`, `data-ok-fill-pattern`, `data-ok-stroke-width`, `data-ok-color-blind-mode`. Core exports `withChartCues`, `chartMarkerNode`, `chartPatternDefs`, `chartPatternNode`, `chartPatternAt`, `chartDiscreteMarkers`, `parseChartDiscreteMarkers`, `chartMarkerShapes` and `chartPatterns`; `ChartNodeTag` gains `pattern`. New stories: Marker shapes, Dashed lines, Pattern fills and Color blind mode. Charts without these options draw exactly as before.

Typography and Link: `maxLines` (1 to 10) clamps text to that many lines with an ellipsis on the last one, and `persistent` keeps the height of every line for shorter text. HTML: `data-ok-max-lines="<n>"` and `data-ok-persistent`, CSS only. Core: `typographyMaxLines`, type `TypographyMaxLines`. A new Typography story, **Text max line**, shows 1 to 4 lines, a link and persistent text.

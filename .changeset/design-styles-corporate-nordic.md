Design styles: the Corporate and Nordic styles as opt-in sheets. Load `@owlky/styles/styles/corporate.css` (CDN `owlky-style-corporate.min.css`) or `nordic.css` after `owlky.css` and set `data-ok-style` on any element. Corporate uses IBM Plex Sans, radius 4 / 8px and subtle shadows, with a blue primary on slate greys (about 2.6 kB gzip). Nordic uses Manrope, radius 10 / 16px and soft shadows, with a teal primary on stone greys (about 2.1 kB gzip). `corporate` and `nordic` are exported from `@owlky/tokens`.
